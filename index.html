🔬 The Science Behind RLI

1. The Flaw of Traditional "Volume"

Standard training models track total volume (Weight × Reps × Sets). However, volume is completely blind to personal structure. If a 60kg athlete and a 100kg athlete both clear 100kg, volume treats them identically, hiding the higher neurological and mechanical stress placed on the lighter athlete.

2. Relative Load Intensity (RLI)

RLI bridges mechanical workload with structural reality. It calculates how hard your moving mass has to work to complete an action:

  • Dynamic Lifts: Volume normalized across biological frames.
  • Static/Isometric holds: Integrated seamlessly via Time Under Tension (Seconds ÷ 10).

3. Advanced Level: Muscle-Isolated RLI

When you input your scale's Muscle % data, the calculation instantly scales your raw load exclusively against force-generating active tissue rather than non-functional fat weight.

Muscle RLI = Total Lifted Volume ÷ Pure Muscle Mass (kg)
